Turn every neighborhood into a carbon farm

CarbonBerry converts garden waste and invasive plants into biochar — carbon locked away in soil for over 1,000 years. One recycled-steel kiln. One app. One community. Real removal, not just reduction.

01 — The Problem

Three problems. One town. Zero connection.

Every neighborhood already produces the raw material for carbon removal — and throws it away in the worst possible way.

Organic waste rots into methane

Green waste landfilled with no oxygen decomposes into methane — roughly 80x more potent than CO2 over 20 years.

Weekly neighborhood waste, as CO2-equivalent0 kg CO2e emitted this visit

Invasives choke ecosystems

Kudzu, water hyacinth and invasive bamboo smother native plants, drain wetlands, and cost communities millions in clearing — usually burned or dumped.

We slow emissions, we don't remove

Recycling and solar reduce new emissions — but the IPCC says we must also pull CO2 back out of the atmosphere. Communities have no accessible tool to do it.

Three problems. One town. Nobody connects the dots — until now.
02 — The Solution

The CarbonBerry Community Kit

A solar-assisted, no-oxygen kiln plus sensors and an offline app — turning yesterday's waste stream into tomorrow's carbon vault.

  • Recycled-steel TLUD kiln — burns biomass top-lit with restricted air: flame cleans itself, char stays solid.
  • Smart lid — temperature and airflow probe confirms pyrolysis happened below the oxygen limit.
  • Weight + moisture sensors — every load measured in, every char weighed out. No guessing, no greenwash.
  • Offline app — works with no internet; logs runs, converts to CO2e, syncs when connected.
  • Biochar soil-pack — charged char goes into gardens, holding water, nutrients and carbon.
  • Community leaderboard — streets, schools and clubs compete to lock the most carbon.

04 — Impact

Small kiln. Compounding carbon.
